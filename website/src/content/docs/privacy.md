**Effective Date:** 2026-08-28

This Privacy Policy explains how Kure Monitor ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you access or use our website, application, and related services.

## 1. Information We Collect

As an open-source, on-premise Kubernetes monitoring tool, Kure Monitor is designed to be privacy-first. When you install Kure Monitor in your cluster:

- **Cluster Data:** Kure Monitor processes your Kubernetes events, pod logs, and manifests locally within your cluster.
- **Telemetry:** We do not collect telemetry, usage metrics, or logs from your self-hosted installations.
- **LLM Integrations:** If you configure Kure Monitor to use cloud-based LLM providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic), data is sent directly from your cluster to those providers according to their respective privacy policies. We do not intermediate this data.

When you visit our website (kuremonitor.com):
- We may collect basic analytics (such as pages visited and browser type) to improve our documentation and website experience.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the minimal website information collected to:
- Improve our website and documentation.
- Respond to your support requests or inquiries.

## 3. Data Security

Since Kure Monitor runs in your environment, securing the application and the underlying cluster is your responsibility. We recommend following Kubernetes security best practices and securing your Kure Monitor dashboard behind authenticated ingresses or VPNs.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Our website may link to third-party services, and our application integrates with third-party LLM providers. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

## 5. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
